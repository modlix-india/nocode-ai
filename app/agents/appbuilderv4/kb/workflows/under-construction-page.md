# under-construction-page

Holding page while a section is being built.

> **Notes:**
> 
> Look at: minimal 3-component tree — a `Grid` (`mainGrid`) as `rootComponent` containing an `Icon` with `iconClass: "fa-solid fa-person-digging"` and a `Label` with `text: "Under Construction"`; page-level `properties.title.name.value` set to `"Under Construction Page"` with `append: false` to override (not append to) the app title.

**Entity type:** `page`
